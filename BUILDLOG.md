# AI assistance log

AI tools were used throughout the project to assist with implementation, testing, debugging, and documentation. Development was directed and reviewed phase by phase by me, and i did chose the final architecture and behaviour, configured integrations, tested the complete workflow and  committed to github.

## Where AI assisted

- Suggested initial FastAPI, database, authentication and test structures.
- Helped implement ingestion, platform-specific draft generation, review states and scheduling.
- Assisted with publisher adapters, idempotency, worker leases, retries, migrations and automated tests.
- Helped diagnose issues such as overly similar platform drafts and accidental external access during a test.
- Assisted in preparing technical documentation and test evidence.

## Developer decisions and validation

The developer selected the capstone scope, divided it into phases, reviewed how each feature worked before implementation, configured and manually verified the Discord integration, required distinct X and LinkedIn outputs, tested the live scheduling workflow and maintained the phase-based Git history. Generated suggestions were adjusted when they did not meet the intended behaviour.
